For companies

Language alone is not enough at work. Misunderstandings rarely come from grammar; they come from hierarchy, directness and unspoken expectations. So every language training is combined with intercultural communication and real situations: meetings, negotiations, business dinners, feedback.

Chinese for German teams

  • Basics for travel and small talk
  • Names, titles and business-card etiquette
  • Business dinners and gifts
  • Pinyin and first characters, with Hanzi Quest

German for Chinese teams

  • Meetings, emails and phone calls in German
  • Presenting and negotiating
  • German work culture: schedules, direct communication, feedback
  • A1 to C1, exam preparation on request

Business English

  • Shared working language for mixed teams
  • Meetings, calls and emails
  • Coordination between German, Chinese and international sites

Intercultural training

  • Hierarchy, decisions and building relationships
  • Understanding direct and indirect communication
  • Negotiation and conflict
  • Preparation for postings or business trips
