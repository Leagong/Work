FAQ

Frequently asked questions

We already have documentation and experienced staff – why isn’t that enough?

In practice we see the same gaps again and again:

  • Experienced employees leave or retire – and their knowledge goes with them.
  • Existing documents are outdated, hard to find, or no longer current after a system upgrade or process changes.
  • Everyone knows only their own part of the process – hardly anyone sees the whole flow from start to finish.
  • Experienced colleagues have their own work and little time to train new people.
  • As a result, new hires take a very long time to master the whole process – far too much time relative to the cost.

I close these gaps: I put the scattered knowledge together into one current, end-to-end process that everyone can look up.

Our group is rolling out a new system – but it doesn’t fit our processes yet. Can you help?

That is exactly where I come in. With a new system or a group-wide rollout, the processes are often not fully thought through, and the requirements don’t match how your business actually runs. I make the gaps between system requirements and day-to-day practice visible, create a clear and reliable picture of the process, and connect it with your existing handbooks and work instructions. Your teams know how to work in the new system – and project management knows where decisions are still needed.

Can’t our own people do this?

Your people have the knowledge – but rarely the time, the method and the neutral view. I document while your team keeps working.
